Our Story

HOUSE OF ZADOCK began with a simple belief: African fashion deserves to be worn with pride, every day. We design and sell clothing that celebrates bold prints, rich colours and the craftsmanship of our heritage.

Each piece is chosen with care, from the fabric to the fit, so you look and feel your best at weddings, celebrations, the office or simply out with friends.
